What you can do

Draw the desired path or import it from a CSV file. REVERSE M13 2D searches candidate planar linkages, displays links and joints, and compares the generated path with the target. How it works

Important limitations

Frequently asked questions

Is M13 a 3D mechanism generator?

No. M13 is the available 2D edition. REVERSE 3D is a separate product in development.

Can I import a target motion?

Yes, by drawing a trajectory or providing CSV points.

Will every trajectory yield a mechanism?

No. Feasibility depends on geometry, selected joints and the search constraints.
