What you can do

Built for technical firms and small teams: plan time-specific tasks, filter priorities, assign up to six professionals, keep private attachments and review progress statistics. Works as an installable web app. Gmail automation is still being tested and should not replace manual checks.

  • Traffic-light alerts and filters

  • Attachments, search and reports
  • Configurable email reminders
  • Statistics by period and assignee

How it works

  1. Sign in to your existing account

  3. Review filters, attachments and statistics regularly
